DATASET: finance
OVERVIEW: This `finance` dataset represents a comprehensive view of a financial institution's operations, encompassing customer management, account tracking, transaction recording, and predictive analytics for marketing. It aims to provide a robust data foundation for understanding customer behavior, managing financial holdings, and driving personalized customer engagement strategies.
TABLES:
  - clientes: The `clientes` table serves as a central repository for storing detailed information about individual customers, primarily for identification, communication, and customer relationship management purposes. It comprehensively captures personal data such as the customer's full name, date of birth, gender, and unique identifiers like CPF and a system-generated client ID, alongside essential contact details including address, email, and phone number.
  - contas: The `contas` table serves as a central repository for detailed information about individual bank accounts. It records key attributes such as a unique account identifier (`id_conta`), the associated client's identifier (`id_cliente`), the account opening date (`data_abertura`), the servicing branch (`agencia`), the current financial balance (`saldo`), and the specific type of account (`tipo_conta`), thereby enabling comprehensive tracking and management of all customer financial holdings.
  - propensao_clientes: The `propensao_clientes` table serves as a predictive analytics output, designed to identify and facilitate targeted engagement with customers who show a high likelihood of accepting specific product offers. It contains key insights such as a "probabilidade_aceitacao" score, the "Next Best Action" (NBA) and "Next Best Offer" (NBO) for each client, a personalized "sugestao_mensagem" for outreach, their "canal_contato_preferido", and essential contact details like "email", "telefone", "nome_completo", and "id_cliente", enabling data-driven and personalized marketing campaigns.
  - transacoes: The `transacoes` table functions as the central ledger for all financial movements, with each row meticulously detailing a specific transaction. It records critical information including a unique transaction identifier, its type (e.g., debit), the monetary value involved, the exact timestamp of the event, a descriptive explanation of the activity, and the specific account associated with the transaction, thereby enabling comprehensive financial tracking, reporting, and analysis of account activities.

DATASET: procurement
OVERVIEW: This `procurement` dataset provides a comprehensive overview of an organization's purchasing activities, spanning the entire procure-to-pay lifecycle. It captures internal item requests, supplier quotations, formal purchase orders, and long-term contractual agreements. The dataset is designed to enable detailed tracking, analysis, and management of procurement processes, supplier performance, cost control, and compliance from the initial demand generation to final order fulfillment and contract adherence.
TABLES:
  - contratos: The `contratos` table serves as a comprehensive ledger for commercial agreements, meticulously detailing each contract at both the overarching agreement level and individual contracted item level. It captures critical information such as unique contract identifiers, specific product or service details including names, quantities, unit prices, and total item values, alongside essential contract attributes like supplier information, validity periods (start, end, and signature dates), payment terms, delivery/billing frequencies, and the overall contract status and estimated value. This table's purpose is to enable detailed management, tracking, and analysis of contractual obligations, product procurement, and supplier relationships throughout their lifecycle.
  - cotacoes: The `cotacoes` table stores detailed individual product quotes received from various suppliers in response to specific quotation requests. It contains comprehensive information for each offer, including the quoted product's name and category, the offering supplier, the quoted value, associated tax rates and discounts, the expected delivery date, and the response status, all linked to a unique quotation request ID. This table is crucial for organizations to analyze and compare supplier proposals to inform their procurement and purchasing decisions.
  - pedidos: The `pedidos` table serves as a detailed record of **purchase order line items**, capturing comprehensive information for each product ordered. It encompasses financial specifics like unit price, quantity, gross and net values, and applied discounts and taxes per item. Furthermore, it tracks logistical data such as expected delivery dates, product attributes including category and unit of measure, and essential metadata about the overarching purchase order, including its ID, issuance date, status, payment terms, associated cost center, and full supplier details, enabling granular procurement analysis.
  - requisicoes: The `requisicoes` table serves as a comprehensive record for internal purchase requisitions, with each row detailing a specific item requested within a larger requisition. It captures granular information about each item, such as product name, category, quantity, unit of measure, estimated costs, and required delivery dates, alongside specific observations. Additionally, the table links these items to their overarching requisition by recording details like the unique requisition ID, the requester's name and department, the date of request, its current status, a general justification, and the suggested cost center, thus providing a complete audit trail for managing and tracking the company's procurement needs.

DATASET: bq_public_data
OVERVIEW: This dataset, `bq_public_data.hacker_news`, represents a comprehensive archive of user-generated content from the Hacker News platform, specifically focusing on "Show HN" type submissions. It functions as a content aggregation and social media dataset, capturing details about user-showcased projects, ideas, and discussions. The data allows for analysis of user engagement, content popularity, and the evolution of technology-related discussions and projects over time.
TABLES:
  - hacker_news: The `hacker_news` table stores individual submissions made to the Hacker News platform, specifically capturing "Show HN" type posts which showcase user-built projects and ideas. Each record contains a unique `id`, the post's `title`, an external `url` if applicable, the detailed `text` content provided by the author (often a project description or discussion), the submitting user's handle (`by`), a `score` indicating its popularity, and the `timestamp` of its creation. This table serves as a comprehensive archive for analyzing user-generated content, discussions, and the evolution of projects featured on Hacker News.

DATASET: moodle_data
OVERVIEW: The `moodle_data` BigQuery dataset represents a comprehensive collection of operational data from a Learning Management System (LMS), specifically Moodle, as indicated by the `mdl_` prefix across all table names. It captures the full lifecycle of educational activities, from course creation and user enrollment to assignment submissions, grading, and overall course completion. The dataset provides granular details on users, courses, course categories, assignments, module activities, and the performance and progress of learners. It is designed to support detailed analytics on learning outcomes, platform engagement, administrative tasks, and educational resource management.
TABLES:
  - mdl_assign: The `mdl_assign` table serves to define and manage individual assignments within a learning management system, storing key metadata such as a unique identifier, the associated course, the assignment's descriptive name, its due date represented as a Unix timestamp, and the maximum possible grade value for that assignment.
  - mdl_assign_submission: The `mdl_assign_submission` table serves as a central repository for tracking student submissions to assignments within the learning management system. Each record uniquely identifies a submission by a specific user for a particular assignment, detailing its current status (e.g., 'submitted') and the timestamp of its last modification, thereby providing essential data for assignment management and progress monitoring.
  - mdl_context: The `mdl_context` table serves as a central registry for defining the hierarchical context of various entities within the system, using `id` as a unique identifier for each context record. It specifies the `contextlevel`, an integer representing the type of context (e.g., system, course, or user), and the `instanceid`, which refers to the specific ID of the entity associated with that context level (e.g., a particular course or user ID). This structure is essential for governing permissions, settings, and data scope across different parts of the application.
  - mdl_course: The `mdl_course` table serves as the primary catalog for all educational courses within the system, detailing their core attributes and lifecycle. It comprehensively stores unique identifiers, categories, full and short names, content summaries, structural formats (e.g., 'topics'), and visibility status. Additionally, it tracks important temporal data like creation, modification, start, and end dates, essential for managing and presenting the platform's diverse learning offerings.
  - mdl_course_categories: The `mdl_course_categories` table serves to define and organize courses within the system by storing metadata for each distinct category. It records a unique `id` for every category, its descriptive `name`, the number of `coursecount` associated with it, and its `visible` status, thereby enabling structured content management and facilitating user navigation through educational offerings.
  - mdl_course_completions: The `mdl_course_completions` table's primary purpose is to record when individual users have successfully completed specific courses within the system. Each entry uniquely identifies a user (`userid`) and the `course` they finished, along with the precise `timecompleted` timestamp, thereby providing a clear history of learner achievement and course completion status.
  - mdl_course_modules: The `mdl_course_modules` table serves as a central registry that defines the specific learning activities and resources comprising a course. It maps individual instances of various module types (e.g., a particular forum, quiz, or assignment) to the `course` they belong to, specifies their designated `section` within that course's structure, and indicates their `visible` status to users. This table is essential for organizing the curriculum, enabling the platform to present a structured and functional list of activities for each course.
  - mdl_course_modules_completion: The `mdl_course_modules_completion` table records the completion status of individual course modules by users. Its purpose is to track and log which specific course module (`coursemoduleid`) has been completed (`completionstate`) by a particular user (`userid`), along with the exact timestamp (`timemodified`) when that completion event was recorded or last updated. This table is crucial for monitoring student progress within courses, assessing engagement with learning materials, and supporting reporting on learning outcomes.
  - mdl_course_sections: The `mdl_course_sections` table is designed to define and organize the logical divisions or topics within each course. It records unique identifiers for each section, links them to their respective courses, specifies their numerical order within that course, and provides a descriptive name, thereby structuring the course content into navigable segments.
  - mdl_enrol: The `mdl_enrol` table in BigQuery configures and manages the enrolment methods available for different courses within the system. Each row defines a specific enrolment method, such as 'manual' enrolment, for a particular `courseid` and indicates its current `status`, thus specifying how users can gain access to or be registered for a course.
  - mdl_grade_grades: The `mdl_grade_grades` table serves as a core component of the grading system, storing individual student grades for specific graded items within the platform. It records the `itemid` and `userid` for each grade entry, capturing both the initial `rawgrade` and the ultimate `finalgrade` awarded to a student for a particular assignment, quiz, or other course component. Its primary purpose is to meticulously track individual student performance, including grade modifications (`usermodified`, `timemodified`), enabling comprehensive grade management, calculation, and reporting across the educational platform.
  - mdl_grade_items: The `mdl_grade_items` table acts as a central catalog for defining and configuring all individual graded activities or assessment components within each course in the learning management system. It comprehensively details each assessable item, such as assignments, by recording its unique identifier, the specific course it belongs to, its descriptive name, the general type and specific module responsible for the activity, and key grading parameters like the grade type and maximum possible score.
  - mdl_role_assignments: The `mdl_role_assignments` table serves to record the associations between users, roles, and the specific contexts in which those roles are granted within the system. This crucial table explicitly defines who (via `userid`) holds what role (via `roleid`) within a particular area or entity of the application (via `contextid`), thereby managing user permissions and access control throughout the platform.
  - mdl_user: The `mdl_user` table serves as the central repository for comprehensive user profile information within the system, likely a learning management platform given the `mdl_` prefix. It stores essential user identification and authentication details such as unique ID, username, hashed password, first name, last name, email address, and authentication method, alongside account status flags (`confirmed`, `deleted`) and administrative timestamps. Furthermore, it captures geographical location data and provides flexibility for extended user demographics through custom fields like gender, age, and education level, facilitating detailed user management and personalization.
  - mdl_user_enrolments: The `mdl_user_enrolments` table records the active participation of individual users (`userid`) in specific course or program instances (`enrolid`). Its purpose is to track the lifecycle of these user-to-enrolment associations, capturing their start and end times (`timestart`, `timeend`) and current operational `status`, thereby managing access and membership within the platform.

DATASET: google_trends
OVERVIEW: The `google_trends` dataset, as represented by the `international_top_terms` table, functions as a global time-series repository for analyzing public interest in search topics. It primarily serves to capture and track the most significant and rapidly rising search queries worldwide on a weekly basis. This dataset is invaluable for understanding global cultural phenomena, major events, and evolving trends, offering insights into what topics are gaining widespread attention and how their popularity shifts over time. It can be categorized as a Public Interest/Trend Analysis dataset.
TABLES:
  - international_top_terms: The `international_top_terms` table serves to track globally trending search terms or topics, identified by the `term` column, and their popularity `rank` on a weekly basis, as indicated by the `week` column. Its primary purpose is to provide time-series insights into what subjects, such as significant sporting events like 'al taawon x al-nassr' observed consistently at rank 1, are gaining widespread international attention.

DATASET: legal
OVERVIEW: This `legal` BigQuery dataset represents a sophisticated Legal Case Management system, specifically tailored for analyzing judicial activities and outcomes within the Brazilian legal framework. It offers a holistic view of legal proceedings, from the initiation of a case involving various companies to the detailed recording of judicial decisions. The dataset facilitates in-depth analysis by linking operational aspects of legal processes—such as involved parties, distribution details, and critical financial contingencies—with the comprehensive metadata and full text of judicial rulings. This integrated approach allows for robust assessment of legal liabilities, evaluation of judicial performance, and tracking of historical trends in legal outcomes for companies operating within this legal environment.
TABLES:
  - decisoes: The `decisoes` table serves as a comprehensive repository for judicial rulings, meticulously documenting individual legal processes and their associated decisions. Each record encapsulates essential metadata such as the process number, type of legal action (`classe`), specific subject (`assunto`), the presiding judge (`magistrado`), and the full judicial hierarchy from `comarca` to `vara`. Crucially, it stores the complete `texto_decisao`, along with the `data_disp`, judicial `instancia`, and the `resultado` of the ruling, providing a detailed dataset for analyzing judicial activity and outcomes within the Brazilian legal system.
  - processos: The `processos` table serves as a comprehensive record of legal cases, detailing specific legal proceedings where various companies (`cod_empresa`) are involved. It tracks key identifiers like the process number and an external link, along with case specifics such as the class, subject, distribution date and location, and details about the involved parties and their participation type. Crucially, the table also captures various financial contingency values and associated risk prognoses for each legal process, enabling detailed analysis of potential legal liabilities and financial exposure for the companies involved.
